**Why it matters:** This approach addresses a critical bottleneck in AI development by ensuring that evaluation standards evolve alongside the models, preventing stagnation.

### [MathCode, Mathematical Coding Agent](https://math-ai-org.github.io/mathcode)

**Hacker News · 2026-08-16**

MathCode is a terminal-based AI coding agent that converts natural language math problems into Lean 4 theorems and attempts formal proofs. It features a persistent Lean REPL, an Obsidian knowledge graph, and agent-mode proving capabilities.

**Why it matters:** This tool bridges the gap between natural language reasoning and formal mathematical verification, offering a powerful new workflow for theorem proving.
